About

I am a Ph.D. student in Systems Engineering at Boston University and an Assistant Professor (on leave) in the Department of Electrical & Electronic Engineering at Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology (RUET), Bangladesh. My research sits at the intersection of control theory, mathematical optimization, and power systems—developing robust, intelligent controllers that keep converter-dominated microgrids and hybrid renewable energy systems dynamically stable.

I earned my M.Sc. (CGPA 3.92/4.00) and B.Sc. (CGPA 3.89/4.00, 2nd in a class of 120) in Electrical & Electronic Engineering from RUET. My research has been published in leading venues including the International Journal of Electrical Power & Energy Systems, Applied Soft Computing, Energy Conversion and Management: X, and IET Renewable Power Generation, and I serve as an active peer reviewer for Elsevier and IEEE publications.

Core Research Thrusts

Control · Optimization · Microgrids
Thrust 01 · Optimization

Multi-Objective Metaheuristics & Online Control

Designing hybrid bio-inspired and convex optimization frameworks for optimal controller tuning, PID neural networks, and economic dispatch in renewable power networks.

Thrust 02 · Robust Control

Disturbance Observer-Based Control (DOBC)

Time-series and nonlinear disturbance observers paired with sliding-mode, fractional-order, and ADRC architectures to reject stochastic wind/solar fluctuations and FDI attacks.

Thrust 03 · Power Systems

Virtual Inertia & Grid-Forming Microgrids

Emulating synthetic inertia via energy storage and grid-forming inverters to arrest rate-of-change-of-frequency (RoCoF) and stabilize low-inertia islanded grids.
