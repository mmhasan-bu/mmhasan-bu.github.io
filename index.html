About

Systems & Control Engineering

I am a Ph.D. student in Systems Engineering at Boston University and an Assistant Professor (on leave) in the Department of Electrical & Electronic Engineering at Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology (RUET), Bangladesh. My research lies at the intersection of control theory, mathematical optimization, and power systems—developing robust, intelligent controllers that preserve stability in converter-dominated microgrids and hybrid renewable energy networks.

I completed my M.Sc. (CGPA 3.92/4.00) and B.Sc. (CGPA 3.89/4.00, ranked 2nd in a class of 120) in Electrical & Electronic Engineering at RUET. My work has been published in journals such as the International Journal of Electrical Power & Energy Systems, Applied Soft Computing, Energy Conversion and Management: X, and IET Renewable Power Generation, and I serve as a regular peer reviewer for Elsevier and IEEE publications.

Core Research Thrusts

Optimization · Observers · Grid Stability
Thrust I · Optimization

Multi-Objective Metaheuristics & Online Control

Designing hybrid bio-inspired and convex optimization frameworks for controller tuning, PID neural networks, and energy management in renewable power networks.

Thrust II · Robust Control

Disturbance Observer-Based Control (DOBC)

Time-series and nonlinear disturbance observers paired with sliding-mode, fractional-order, and ADRC architectures to reject stochastic renewable fluctuations and FDI attacks.

Thrust III · Power Systems

Virtual Inertia & Grid-Forming Microgrids

Emulating synthetic inertia via energy storage and grid-forming inverters to arrest rate-of-change-of-frequency (RoCoF) and stabilize low-inertia islanded grids.
