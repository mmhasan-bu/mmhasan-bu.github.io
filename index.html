Systems & Control Engineering

Architecting resilient control for low-inertia power grids.

I am Md. Mahmudul Hasan—a Ph.D. researcher in Systems Engineering at Boston University and an Assistant Professor (on leave) in Electrical & Electronic Engineering at RUET. My research bridges mathematical optimization, nonlinear disturbance observers, and virtual inertia emulation to stabilize renewable-dominated microgrids.

01.

Research Agenda

Investigating the theoretical and algorithmic foundations of frequency/voltage stability in converter-dominated power systems.

Domain I · Optimization

Multi-Objective Metaheuristics & Online Control

Hybrid bio-inspired and convex optimization architectures for optimal controller tuning, PID neural network initialization, and microgrid energy management.

Domain II · Robust Control

Disturbance Observer-Based Control (DOBC)

Time-series and nonlinear disturbance observers coupled with sliding-mode, fractional-order, and ADRC laws to counter stochastic generation and FDI cyber-attacks.

Domain III · Power Systems

Virtual Inertia & Grid-Forming Microgrids

Emulating synthetic rotational inertia via energy storage and grid-forming inverters to arrest rate-of-change-of-frequency (RoCoF) during islanded contingencies.
